About us

I wanted a product I'd take myself, without a second thought.

Sport and healthy eating have been part of my life from the start. I've always been interested in vitamins, plant extracts, and how we can get more out of our bodies, how we can function even better.

The price of today's pace

The pace of today's world affects all of us. Long hours, constant stress, too little sleep, meals eaten in a rush, often ultra-processed. With days like this, it gets harder and harder to give our bodies everything they need.

What I was missing

I noticed how hard it is to find products on the market that are genuinely good quality, that don't cut corners on the actives, and whose actives are actually lab-tested.

That's why I created Nutrimind

I wanted products that are fully transparent. I know exactly what's in them, where it comes from and what quality it is. We put the actives together so they work well as a system.

  • Exact doses on the label, no proprietary blends
  • Tested in Eurofins' accredited lab
  • Developed and made in Hungary

My goal

To bring a healthy lifestyle to as many people as possible. To do that, I want to offer something you can actually rely on.

Péter Sebők, founder of Nutrimind
