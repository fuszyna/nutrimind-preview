Ordering & delivery

Home delivery by GLS courier

Orders are delivered to your home by GLS courier within 1 working day of ordering.

  • Delivery fee: HUF 1,590
  • FREE on orders over HUF 19,000

GLS parcel pickup point

Your parcel is delivered to the GLS parcel locker of your choice within 1–2 working days of ordering. You can find pickup points with the GLS parcel locker finder.

  • Delivery fee: HUF 1,190
  • FREE on orders over HUF 19,000

Home delivery by Magyar Posta (MPL)

Orders are delivered to your home by Magyar Posta (MPL) within 7–10 working days of ordering.

  • Delivery fee: HUF 6,990 (HUF 1,990 delivery + HUF 5,000 administration fee)
  • On orders over HUF 18,000, the HUF 5,000 administration fee applies.

GLS delivers your parcel to your home within 1 working day of your order, or to a pickup point within 1–2 working days.

Cash on delivery (+HUF 590)

With cash on delivery, the full purchase price and the delivery fee are paid to the courier on delivery, in cash or by bank card.

Card payment (free)

If you choose card payment, you can pay quickly and securely through Stripe or OTP SimplePay by entering your card details, or by using your existing SimplePay account.

Bank transfer (free)

After you place your order, we e-mail you the details needed for the transfer and ship your order as soon as the amount arrives.
