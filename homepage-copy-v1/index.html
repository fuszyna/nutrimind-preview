How do I take the Day and Night products together?

Take the Day formula in the morning, ideally after breakfast, and the Night formula in the evening before bed. They are designed to work as one 24-hour routine and can be taken together safely.

Are Nutrimind products vegan, gluten- and lactose-free?

Yes. Every product is made with 100% natural plant-based actives and is vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, sugar-free and contains no preservatives.

How long before I notice a difference?

Everyone is different. Plant actives work best with consistent daily use, so we recommend at least 30 days, which is why our two- and three-month supplies are the most popular choice.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Every product is covered by our 60-day results guarantee. Take it daily for 60 days; if you're not satisfied after that, contact us and we'll refund you.

How fast is delivery, and is it discreet?

We ship with GLS and most orders arrive the next working day. Delivery is free on orders over HUF 19,000, and every order is sent in plain, discreet packaging.

Where are your products made and tested?

All Nutrimind products are developed and manufactured in Hungary in an ISO 22000, HACCP and GMP certified facility, with active ingredients tested by Eurofins, an accredited laboratory.
