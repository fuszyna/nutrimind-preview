Know the signs

What does a mineral deficiency cause?

When fluid, potassium and magnesium run low, for example after a long night, the body tends to tell you:

  • Low blood pressureFeeling light-headed or dizzy when you stand up.
  • HeadacheA dull, persistent headache that coffee doesn't fix.
  • Difficulty concentratingFoggy thinking and slower reactions through the day.
  • Muscle crampsCramps, twitches or heavy, tired muscles.
  • TirednessRunning on empty, even after a full night's sleep.
  • Irregular heartbeatA racing or fluttering heart when fluids run low.

If symptoms are strong or don't pass, talk to a doctor. DROP is a food supplement, not a medicine.
